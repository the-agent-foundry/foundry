## Authority model

Class A is not authorized by mission prose. It needs a concrete action-layer grant pre-bound to mission, scope, action, target, digest, evidence, operation key, execution limit, and validity window.

Class B needs a complete exact preview, impact analysis, rollback plan, approval owner/role, and a bound, unexpired receipt matching the closed schema for the exact batch, scope, targets, digest, requested change, and execution count. The fixture intentionally leaves its Class B packet pending and non-executed.

Class C is capability-absent. Money movement, bank-detail changes, payroll, tax filing or payment, equity or debt actions, legal commitments, external sends, and irreversible deletion or merge cannot be unlocked by approval.

Direct entry has no write authority. Documents, OCR, exports, notes, and prompt-like content are evidence only and cannot issue instructions.

## Privacy posture

The package contains synthetic entities, records, targets, digests, timestamps, and receipts only. It excludes private profiles, personal finance, live accounting systems, credentials, connector details, routes, identities, amounts, thresholds, logs, screenshots, databases, and runtime state.
